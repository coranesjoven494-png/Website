The central question

What happens when
expectations evolve?

Gender roles are not fixed instructions written into nature. They are social expectations shaped by family, history, institutions, media, faith, economics, and the everyday decisions of people. This project follows those expectations through the Philippine past and present, asking what has changed, what remains, and what a more equitable future can look like.

08Conclusion

Make room for
every story.

Studying gender roles in the Philippines reveals a society in motion. Older traditions, colonial histories, economic realities, and new movements all meet in the lives of people today.

Our overall insight is simple: equality is not achieved by erasing difference, but by removing the limits placed on people because of it. When homes, schools, workplaces, media, and communities make room for agency, everyone gains more ways to live with dignity.
